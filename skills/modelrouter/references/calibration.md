# Measure improvement without rewarding shortcuts

Cost objective: total measured cost per accepted task, subject to unchanged quality/verification. Raw total tokens may increase while billed credits decrease; latency may improve while cost rises. Keep all metrics separate. Never claim savings from an illustrative price calculation.

Keep task buckets comparable: phase/domain, scope, risk, acceptance difficulty, tool environment, candidate version and cache accounting. Include the parent, every child, failed attempts, repairs and reviews. Unknown telemetry remains null. Do not add reasoning tokens to total output again if already included, or add cached input to total input again.

Observe ordinary authorized work first; no extra paid benchmarks by default. Opt-in calibration may compare an incumbent and candidate on frozen, representative tasks and a protected grading protocol. Keep holdout acceptance separate from author feedback; hiding a test string alone is not an OS security boundary. A reviewer verdict without execution/source evidence is not an accepted outcome.

Record: exact host-observed model and effort; task bucket; quality result and independent evidence reference; number of attempts/repair passes; parent/child totals; pricing version/service tier/currency when present; latency; date and model snapshot if available. Do not store secrets, raw personal data or full prompts in a cross-project cache.

For explicit bounded local recording and matched route comparisons, read [metrics.md](metrics.md). Its helper stores caller-supplied observations only. A ready comparison is evidence about its recorded matched sample, not proof of causality or universal savings.

Profiles can be updated by an authorized coordinator using reviewed evidence, not by implementation agents. A routing helper does not verify the truth of profile claims. Avoid immediate global promotion from one task. Use several representative successes plus a held-out regression check for material promotion; counts are operational heuristics, not statistical proof. Where matched cost/quality evidence is insufficient, keep the documented incumbent or label a limited canary.

Compare failure-inclusive cost of accepted work. Penalize repeated repairs and substantive escaped defects, not an honest BLOCKED result caused by a truly unavailable environment. An integrity incident suspends the route and acceptance until trusted re-evaluation. Do not select for high PASS rates obtained by weaker tests or skipped cases.

On a substantive regression, restore the prior evidence-backed route for affected task classes while preserving actual failure records. Do not discard older capable models by version number. Revisit role fit when workload or model behavior changes. No claims of learned self-optimization until real measurements exist.
